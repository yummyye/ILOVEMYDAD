A SPECIAL DAY · JUST FOR YOU

Dear Dad, ♡

Happy birthday to the most wonderful dad in my little world!

Thank you for every little thing you do, even the things I might not always notice or know how to put into words. Thank you for your hard work, your patience, your care, and for always trying your best for our family.

I know I may not say it every day, but I appreciate you more than you know. Your presence means so much to me. You make ordinary days feel safer, warmer, and more special just by being there.

For your new age, I hope life gives you plenty of reasons to smile. I hope you stay healthy, feel loved, find happiness in small things, and enjoy all the good things you deserve. Please remember to rest, too. You deserve care just as much as you give it.

Whatever happens as I grow up, a part of me will always be your little kid. I'll keep cheering for you, being proud of you, and loving you in my own little ways.

I love you, Dad. Today, tomorrow, and always. ♡

Your little girl ✿
